Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Pixelrealism ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads short videos of a physics sandbox simulation to the TikTok account of its owner. This policy explains what data the app handles.

What data the app uses

  • When you connect your TikTok account, TikTok gives the app an access token and basic profile information (your display name and account ID). These are used only to upload and publish videos you choose.
  • The app uploads only video files and captions that you select.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or read your messages, contacts, followers, comments or viewing history.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, except TikTok itself as required to upload your videos.

Data retention

Access tokens are kept only as long as your TikTok account stays connected. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings under Security > Manage app permissions, which removes the app's access immediately.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
